## How accurate are the estimates?

Tested on the 758 rows that already have measured kcal, protein, fat and carbs:

- **Carbs by difference** vs measured carbs (655 rows with usable inputs): median error 0.0 g, 90th percentile 0.2 g per 100 g.
- **Carbs by difference with the food group's typical ash** (used only when a row's ash is missing or corrupt; 734 rows): median error 0.5 g, 90th percentile 2.3 g per 100 g.
- **kcal from Atwater factors** vs measured kcal: median error 0.3 kcal, 90th percentile 2.6 kcal per 100 g.
